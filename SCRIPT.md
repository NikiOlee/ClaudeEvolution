# Script (drama-3, narrator = Ethan, mascot = funny talking monkey voice)

1. **NARRATOR** — `[hushed, reverent, nature documentary gravitas] In the beginning, there was the ocean. And in it, a tadpole. [pause] Scientists say tadpoles don't live in the ocean. [dry, deadpan] This one didn't read the memo.`

2. **MASCOT** — `[tiny, squeaky, cheerful] Hi! Am I famous yet?`

3. **NARRATOR** — `[flat, unimpressed] Not yet. [pause] [grand, swelling] But through ancient pressures, it evolves. Behold: the fish. [proud, hushed] A powerful sense of direction, [sighing] which it does not use.`

4. **NARRATOR** — `[whispering, thrilled like spotting a rare animal] Boldly, it crawls onto land. The lizard. [serious, grave] It has no plan. It has four legs. Neither is helping.`

5. **NARRATOR** — `[building, booming] Generations pass. It grows. And grows. [majestic, triumphant] Behold: the dinosaur! [proud] So large, so confident, so certain nothing could possibly go wrong.`

6. **NARRATOR** — `[solemn, hushed reverence] The mighty king gazes at a beautiful new star, which is getting [voice cracking, sudden panic, shouting] bigger! Why is it getting bigger?! Oh no, oh no, oh no— [flat, exhausted, deadpan] ...Well. That was the dinosaurs.`

7. **NARRATOR** — `[gentle, hopeful, quiet dawn] But life finds a way. From a small hole, a rat emerges. [whispering] Humble. Hungry. [amused] Strangely familiar eyes.`

8. **NARRATOR** — `[amazed, warm] Time passes. The rat becomes a monkey.`

9. **MASCOT** — `[squeaky, indignant] Hey! I'm still basically a rat!`

10. **NARRATOR** — `[calm, unbothered] The monkey wishes to be heard. We do not wish to hear it. [stirring, rising majesty] Then, it stands. The human. [reverent] Excellent posture.`

11. **NARRATOR** — `[tender, hushed] Here, it meets its mate, and offers a hand in friendship. [excited whisper] A high five.`

12. **NARRATOR** — `[long pause] [flat disbelief, deadpan] And the mate is a rat again. Immediately. [weary, resigned] Millions of years of evolution, undone by one high five.`

13. **MASCOT** — `[tiny, squeaky, satisfied] Squeak!`
